Group A: The Advanced AI & GIS Analytics Portal
This group moves beyond basic regression into computer vision, explainable AI, and macro-economic forecasting.

🚀 Upgraded Feature Set
Feature 1: Computer Vision Property Tagger (Image ML)

What it is: When a user uploads photos of a house/land, Group A's backend runs it through a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) or a lightweight image classification model (using PyTorch or TensorFlow).

The Complexity: The AI automatically categorizes the image tags (e.g., "Modern Interior", "Bare Land", "Needs Structural Renovation", "Commercial Frontage") and feeds these tags directly into the pricing model as premium multipliers.

Feature 2: Explainable AI (XAI) Dashboard

What it is: Professors hate "black box" AI where numbers just appear. Group A will implement SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) or feature-importance tracking.

The Complexity: When the AI estimates a land plot's value, the dashboard renders a visual chart breaking down exactly why it chose that price (e.g., +15% due to 20ft road access, -10% due to distance from city center, +5% due to high commercial zoning).

Feature 3: Geospatial Kriging & Price Contour Maps

What it is: Using GeoPandas and spatial interpolation algorithms, Group A generates a live, color-coded heat-contour map over the entire city.

The Complexity: It predicts land prices for areas where no historical sales data exists yet, creating a smooth visual gradient of "High Value to Low Value" zones on their Leaflet interface.

Feature 4: Automated Fiscal & Municipal Tax Simulator

What it is: An engine that automatically calculates local government land taxes, capital gains taxes, and registration fees based on the plot's valuation and the user's demographic profile.

and an immutable bidding ledger.
🚀 Upgraded Feature SetFeature 1: Interactive Land Polygon Drawing (Leaflet.draw)What it is: Sellers don't just drop a single pin; they use a 
custom UI brush tool to draw the exact geometric boundary borders of their plot directly onto the satellite map.The Complexity: Group B's 
frontend uses geospatial geometry math (Turf.js) to instantly calculate the total land area, perimeter, and aspect ratio from those coordinates,
 sending the raw spatial boundary polygon data to the database.
 Feature 2: Proximity-Based Live Routing & Site NavigationWhat it is: Integrates the Leaflet Routing Machine engine directly into the 
 mobile-responsive view.The Complexity: When a buyer finds a plot of land they want to physically inspect, the app hooks into their phone's 
 live GPS and draws a real-time, turn-by-turn driving or walking route from their current feet to the exact boundary gateway of the land plot.
 Feature 3: Multi-State Escrow & Bid Negotiation LedgerWhat it is: A secure, state-machine binding architecture for buying land.
 The Complexity: Buyers don't just message a seller; they submit a binding "Earnest Money Offer." The system locks their deposit token into an e
 scrow table, generates a legal bid ladder (Offers, Counter-Offers, Rejections), and tracks the contractual state (Bidded $\rightarrow$ Under 
 Review $\rightarrow$ Escrow Locked $\rightarrow$ Transferred).
 Feature 4: Spatial Anti-Fraud GeofencingWhat it is: Validates listings using hardware constraints.The Complexity: To list a property as 
 "Owner Verified," the seller must physically walk onto the property and tap a button. The app checks their smartphone's hardware GPS coordinates 
 against the drawn land polygon. If they aren't physically standing inside the boundaries, the listing is flagged as unverified.
